**Project name:** TerraTrace – Climate & Nature Navigator

**Team:** FarmSense

**Tagline (short):** Climate and nature risk for Apetit in euros, tonnes and next-week actions – every number traceable and expert-approved.

**About:**
TerraTrace turns Apetit's fragmented climate, farming, nature and transport data into decisions.
It forecasts contract-crop yields for 2027–2031 and for 2041–2060 under three climate scenarios,
prices the shortfall in euros, and shows how much irrigation and two-region sourcing reduce the risk.
It ranks catchments by phosphorus and nitrogen pressure on waters (SBTN step 2), calculates
transport emissions (Scope 3 categories 4 and 9) automatically and fixes missing sea legs.
A Copilot answers questions in plain English with evidence. Every number has a Source button, every
data correction is logged, and nothing is exported until a reviewer approves it.
Checks: our 2023–2024 transport totals match Apetit's public dev inventory; 8/8 missing legs found;
the yield model beats the baseline for every crop in a 2023–2026 back-test.

**Built with:** Python, pandas, NumPy, JavaScript (no framework), SVG charts, Featherless AI (OpenAI-compatible API)

**AI tools used:** Claude (coding assistant), Featherless AI (Copilot answers)

**Partner technologies:** Featherless AI
